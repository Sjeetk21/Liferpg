Consistency Score Formula

Your Consistency Score is calculated based on daily checklist executions over the selected time range:

Score = [Completed / (Completed + Missed)] * 100%

Daily/Weekly habits that are completed add to Completed. Habits that you fail to mark or mark as missed (❌) add to Missed. Maintain focus to keep this score near 100%!
